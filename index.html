Gold Buying & Purity Guide

Essential standards for smart jewellery purchases

BIS 6-Digit HUID Hallmark

Verify the mandatory BIS triangle logo, purity grade (e.g., 22K916), and unique 6-character alphanumeric HUID code using the official BIS Care Android app before buying.

Karat & Fineness Standards
  • • 24K (999): 99.9% pure gold used for investment coins and bars.
  • • 22K (916): 91.6% gold alloyed for strength; standard for traditional ornaments.
  • • 18K (750): 75.0% gold; preferred for diamond-studded jewellery.
  • • 14K (585): 58.5% gold; durable for everyday lightweight wear.
Making Charges & Wastage (VA)

Making charges cover artisan labor, while Value Addition (VA) accounts for gold lost during crafting. Always check net gold weight excluding stone weight.
